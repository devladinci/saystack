Try it
  1. Hold the mic. The words stream into the middle; let go and they drop into your draft.
  2. Keep holding. A long dictation shrinks twice, then scrolls with the newest line at the bottom.
  3. While holding, slide your finger away from the mic to cancel.
  4. Tap the speaker under the long reply. Tap any word to jump there.
  5. The blur stays while it reads. Stop it in the player, or let it finish, and the reply settles back.

Speech is simulated; nothing uses your microphone. The long dictation plays at 1.5× so you don't have to hold for half a minute.
